Company: Meridian Health Systems
Job Title: Senior Software Engineer - Clinical Platform
Job ID: MHS-9001

Meridian Health Systems builds clinical software used by clinicians to manage patient
records and deliver remote care to patients at home.

Responsibilities:
- Build backend services for clinical and patient-facing workflows using Python and Django.
- Work with relational databases containing healthcare records and clinical audit trails.
- Review pull requests and give other engineers actionable, specific feedback.
- Maintain code quality through peer review and testing across the clinical platform.
- Own the quality bar for the patient-records codebase.
- Integrate telemedicine video consultation and clinical report generation features.
- Work directly with clinicians and product stakeholders on workflow requirements.

Requirements:
- Python, Django, REST APIs, SQL, and PostgreSQL.
- Strong code-review skills on a multi-person engineering team.
- Experience building healthcare, telemedicine, or clinical workflow software.
- Familiarity with WebSockets, Redis, WebRTC, or AWS is a plus.
- Experience with HIPAA, FHIR, or EHR integrations is preferred but not required.
